Pitcher availability today

Rest days follow Little League pitch-count rules for ages 7–16: 66 or more pitches needs 4 days, 51–65 needs 3, 36–50 needs 2, 21–35 needs 1. Daily maximums depend on age (75 for ages 9–10, 85 for 11–12). Check the current rulebook if your league changes them.
